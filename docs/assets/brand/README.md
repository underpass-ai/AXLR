# Transparent Spectrum logos

| Product | Vector | Raster | Dimensions |

| KMP | [SVG](kmp-spectrum.svg) | [PNG](kmp-spectrum.png) | 712 × 320 |

All exports have transparent backgrounds and four horizontal Spectrum bars stacked vertically to form a square. The bars have equal widths and straight ends, with uniform transparent gaps. The block sits below the wordmark, aligned with its right edge. Each product uses its own palette, in the following top-to-bottom order for the bars and left-to-right order for MADE and AXLR's letters:

| Product | Copper | Gold | Brown | Sand |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| KMP | `#C96B2C` | `#D89A3D` | `#7A5230` | `#D8C3A5` |
| MADE | `#B6633A` | `#D98545` | `#5A3E2B` | `#BFA27A` |
| AXLR | `#B85C38` | `#D97B2D` | `#6B4A2E` | `#C9B08C` |

KMP retains its color sweep by row: copper, copper, gold, brown, sand, sand. No additional shades or background colors are baked into the exports. Keep the bars with the complete wordmark and preserve each image's aspect ratio.

All six files are generated by the dependency-free [pixel wordmark renderer](../../../tools/pixelart/README.md). Edit its terminal-art definitions to change lettering, color or layout, then regenerate both formats from the same source. CI verifies the committed exports.

KMP retains its current pixel shapes. MADE retains its distinct pixel lettering and omits the background, tagline and “by Underpass” line. AXLR's supplied [original raster](../axlr-wordmark.png) remains as a reference; its letters and stepped outlines are now reconstructed as vector shapes. Every SVG contains only rectangles and polygons, with no fonts or embedded bitmap.
